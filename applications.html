Section D

Real-World Applications

Five products that beginners already use daily, each traced back to the Week 1 concepts quietly running underneath.

01

Search Engines

TokenizationStop-word RemovalTF-IDFWord EmbeddingsContextual Embeddings
Relevant concepts & why neededA search query and billions of web pages both have to be broken into tokens and turned into comparable vectors before "relevance" can even be defined. TF-IDF ranks pages by distinctive keyword overlap; embeddings let the engine match a query to a page that expresses the same idea in different words.
Expected benefitsFaster, more relevant results; matches based on meaning rather than exact keyword strings, so a search for "affordable laptops" also surfaces pages that say "budget notebooks".
02

Chatbots & Virtual Assistants

TokenizationLemmatizationContextual EmbeddingsTransformer-based Representations
Relevant concepts & why neededUser messages are informal and grammatically loose, so lemmatization and cleaning normalise phrasing before intent detection. Transformer-based contextual embeddings let the assistant track pronouns and references across a multi-turn conversation ("it", "that one") correctly.
Expected benefitsMore natural, context-aware conversations; fewer misunderstood requests; the ability to handle follow-up questions without the user repeating themselves.
03

Sentiment Analysis

Text CleaningN-GramsWord EmbeddingsNeural Language Models
Relevant concepts & why neededCleaning must be careful not to strip emphasis or negation ("not good" is the opposite of "good"), which is exactly why N-grams and context-aware embeddings — not single-word Bag of Words — are preferred here: they preserve the short phrase a sentiment word depends on.
Expected benefitsBrands and platforms can automatically gauge public opinion on products, reviews, and social posts at a scale no human team could read manually, and react faster to shifting sentiment.
04

Machine Translation

Sentence SegmentationTokenizationWord EmbeddingsTransformer-based Representations
Relevant concepts & why neededSentence segmentation gives the model a coherent unit to translate at a time; embeddings and transformer attention let the model align meaning across two languages with completely different word order and grammar, rather than translating word-for-word.
Expected benefitsFluent, context-appropriate translations that handle idioms and long-range grammatical agreement far better than older phrase-substitution systems.
05

Text Summarization

Sentence SegmentationTF-IDFContextual EmbeddingsTransformer-based Representations
Relevant concepts & why neededExtractive summarizers use TF-IDF-style scoring to rank the most representative sentences in a document; abstractive summarizers use transformer-based contextual understanding to generate new sentences that compress the source's meaning.
Expected benefitsTurns long articles, meeting transcripts, or research papers into a few readable sentences, saving readers time while retaining the key information.
