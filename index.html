WHY

Why a "corpus notebook" instead of a slide deck?

NLP is easiest to understand as a chain of transformations applied to text, not as a list of isolated definitions. Every artifact here is written to answer the same five questions a beginner actually asks: what is this, why does it exist, how does it work, when should I reach for it, and what does it cost me? Where a technique only makes sense next to its alternative — stemming beside lemmatization, TF beside TF-IDF — the comparison tables in Section B carry that weight instead of repeating it in every card.
